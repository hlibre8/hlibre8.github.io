Research themes

I am interested in how processing and chemistry control structure, properties, and performance. My experience spans ceramics and coatings, electrochemical materials, polymer science, additive manufacturing, modeling, and image analysis.

01

Extreme-Environment Ceramics & Coatings

UHTCs, ceramic-matrix composites, pyrocarbon interphases, environmental barrier coatings, high-temperature degradation, and processing.

02

Electrochemical & Functional Materials

Electrocatalysis, redox-active polymers, flow batteries, energy conversion, and electrochemical characterization.

03

Polymer Science, Additive Manufacturing & Digital Tools

RAFT polymerization, PISA, 3D printing, COMSOL modeling, microscopy automation, and computer vision.
