Hi, I’m Hossein.

I’m a ceramic engineering and chemistry student at Missouri S&T, and this site is where I share the projects, ideas, and interests that keep me curious. My research spans extreme-environment ceramics, electrochemical systems and electrosynthesis, polymer science, and digital tools for materials science.

Outside the lab, I’m drawn to glass both as something I can shape by hand and as the lens through which I photograph the world. Here, you’ll find a mix of my research and some of the things I make and notice along the way.
