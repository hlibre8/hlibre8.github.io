Hi, I’m Hossein.

I’m an undergraduate student in Ceramic Engineering and Chemistry at Missouri S&T. My research spans extreme-environment ceramics, electrochemical systems and electrosynthesis, polymer science, and digital materials tools.

I’m an undergraduate researcher at the Kummer Institute for Resource Sustainability (KIRCS), advised by Prof. Shelley Minteer. I also conduct research in the Ultra-High Temperature Ceramics Group as a Dean’s Undergraduate Research Scholar, co-advised by Prof. Greg Hilmas and Prof. Jason Lonergan.
